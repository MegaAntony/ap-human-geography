2.Topic 2.1Skill 1.AWhere People Live · p. 55
A geographer maps every household in a county to show which areas are crowded, sparsely settled, or empty. A second geographer divides the county's population by its square miles. What are the two geographers measuring?
💡 Clue

Distribution = the PATTERN (where). Density = the NUMBER per area (how many).

📖 Answer, trap & book proof

Answer: A. The first measures population distribution; the second measures population density.

Why: Distribution is the spread of people across the earth (shown on a map); density is the average population per square mile or kilometer.

⚠️ Trap: B swaps the two terms. On the AP exam the two are always paired, so read which one is a pattern and which is a ratio.

Book proof (AMSCO p. 55): “Population distribution is the pattern of human settlement-the spread of people across the earth.”
