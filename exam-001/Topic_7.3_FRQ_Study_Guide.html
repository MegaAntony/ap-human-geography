📈 Section 1: Economic Measures & Human Development Index (Student pp. 463–465 / Key p. 85)
TOPIC 7.3 • GNI PER CAPITA & PPP Define
📖 Student Book: Page 464 (Economic Indicators) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Define Gross National Income (GNI) per capita and explain why Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) adjustments are necessary.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"GNI per capita is the total income earned by a country's citizens and businesses divided by total population. PPP adjustments are necessary because the cost of living varies globally; PPP adjusts income metrics to show what money actually buys locally in terms of goods and services."
💡 Memory Hook: GNI = Total national money $\div$ population. PPP = Adjusting for local grocery and rent costs!
TOPIC 7.3 • HUMAN DEVELOPMENT INDEX (HDI) Identify
📖 Student Book: Pages 464–465 (HDI Framework) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Identify the THREE core components that the United Nations combines to calculate the Human Development Index (HDI).
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"1. Decent Standard of Living: GNI per capita adjusted for Purchasing Power Parity (PPP).
2. Long and Healthy Life: Life expectancy at birth.
3. Access to Knowledge: Mean years of schooling for adults and expected years of schooling for children."
💡 Memory Hook: HDI = Money (GNI) + Health (Life Expectancy) + Brains (Schooling)!
⚖️ Section 2: Measuring Inequality: Gini Coefficient & Gender Inequality Index (Student pp. 466–470 / Key p. 85)
STUDENT BOOK IN-TEXT MAP Explain
📖 Student Book: Page 466 (Gini Coefficient by Country Map) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Explain how the Gini coefficient measures income inequality and describe the correlation between industrialization, middle class size, and Gini scores.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"The Gini coefficient measures income distribution from 0.0 (perfect equality) to 1.0 (complete inequality). Countries that have undergone industrialization and economic development tend to have a larger middle class, which directly correlates with a lower Gini coefficient (closer to 0.0)."
💡 Memory Hook: 0.0 = Everyone equal (happy). 1.0 = King takes everything (unequal). Big middle class = Low Gini!
STUDENT BOOK IN-TEXT TABLE Describe
📖 Student Book: Pages 468–469 (GII World Rankings Table) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Describe the THREE dimensions assessed by the Gender Inequality Index (GII) and explain the significance of a low GII score (such as Switzerland).
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"1. Reproductive Health: Maternal mortality ratio and adolescent fertility rate.
2. Empowerment: Percentage of parliamentary/legislative seats held by women and female secondary education rates.
3. Labor Market Participation: Percentage of adult women in the formal workforce.

Significance: A low GII score (closer to 0.0) signifies high gender equality, lower maternal mortality, and expanded female educational opportunities."
💡 Memory Hook: GII = Health (Mothers surviving) + Power (Women in government/school) + Jobs (Women working).
TOPIC 7.3 • SCALE COMPARISON Compare
📖 Student Book: Pages 465, 468 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Compare the score interpretation of the Human Development Index (HDI) with the Gender Inequality Index (GII).
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"For the Human Development Index (HDI), a score closer to 1.0 is the most desirable (highest socio-economic development); for the Gender Inequality Index (GII), a score closer to 0.0 is the most desirable (lowest gender disparity)."
💡 Memory Hook: HDI = Want High (1.0). GII = Want Zero inequality (0.0).
🔍 Section 3: Limitations of Metrics & The Informal Economy (Student pp. 464–465 / Key p. 85)
TOPIC 7.3 • METRIC LIMITATIONS Explain
📖 Student Book: Page 464 (Evaluating GDP Limitations) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Explain ONE major limitation of using national-level economic indicators (such as GDP per capita) to evaluate human well-being.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"National averages conceal internal regional disparities (such as wealth concentrated in coastal cities while rural villages remain impoverished) and fail to measure human rights, environmental quality, or distribution of wealth."
💡 Memory Hook: A country can have high average GDP because of 5 billionaires, while most citizens live in poverty!
TOPIC 7.3 • INFORMAL ECONOMY Explain
📖 Student Book: Page 465 (Formal vs. Informal Economy) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 85 (PDF Page 93)
Explain why the informal economy is widespread in less developed countries (LDCs) and why it is excluded from GDP statistics.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"The informal economy (unregistered street vending, cash day labor) is widespread in LDCs where formal salaried jobs are scarce; it is omitted from GDP calculations because cash transactions are unrecorded, untaxed, and unregulated by the government."
💡 Memory Hook: Informal = Cash under the table, street markets, no taxes, no official records.
