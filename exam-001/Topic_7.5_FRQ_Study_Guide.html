🪜 Section 1: Rostow's Stages of Economic Growth Model (Student pp. 484–485 / Key p. 89)
TOPIC 7.5 • ROSTOW'S 5 STAGES Identify
📖 Student Book: Page 484 (Development Theories Table) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Identify the FIVE stages of Rostow’s Stages of Economic Growth model in chronological order.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"1. Stage 1: Traditional Society (subsistence farming, limited technology).
2. Stage 2: Preconditions for Take-Off (commercial farming, infrastructure investment).
3. Stage 3: Take-Off (rapid industrialization in leading sectors, high urbanization).
4. Stage 4: Drive to Maturity (technology diffuses to all sectors, economic diversification).
5. Stage 5: High Mass Consumption (consumer goods, services, high median income)."
💡 Memory Hook: Traditional $\rightarrow$ Pre-conditions $\rightarrow$ Take-Off $\rightarrow$ Drive $\rightarrow$ High Mass Consumption!
TOPIC 7.5 • ROSTOW TAKE-OFF STAGE Describe
📖 Student Book: Page 484 (Stage 3 Take-Off) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Describe the primary economic characteristics of Rostow’s "Take-Off" stage (Stage 3).
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Stage 3 (Take-Off) is characterized by rapid industrial growth in a few leading sectors (e.g., textiles, food processing), breakthrough technology adoption, rapid rural-to-urban migration, and high rates of capital investment."
💡 Memory Hook: The rocket launches! Factories start booming and farm workers flood into city factories.
OFFICIAL CHAPTER 19 FRQ • CRITIQUE Explain
📖 Student Book: Page 485 & Page 494 (Chapter 19 FRQ Part C) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 91 (PDF Page 99)
Explain ONE major critique or limitation of Rostow’s Stages of Economic Growth model.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Rostow’s model is Eurocentric and assumes all countries follow the exact historical timeline of Western nations, while ignoring how colonial exploitation, structural debt, and natural resource deficits prevent developing countries from independently climbing the ladder."
💡 Memory Hook: Rostow assumes every country has the same advantages America had—ignoring the lasting damage of colonialism!
🌐 Section 2: Wallerstein's World Systems Theory & Dependency (Student pp. 484–486 / Key p. 89)
TOPIC 7.5 • WALLERSTEIN WORLD SYSTEMS Explain
📖 Student Book: Page 484 (Dependency Models) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Explain the core premise of Wallerstein’s World Systems Theory (Core-Periphery Model).
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Wallerstein’s theory is a structural dependency model arguing that the global economy is a single interconnected capitalist hierarchy where wealthy Core countries exploit Periphery countries for cheap raw materials and low-wage labor, maintaining global inequality."
💡 Memory Hook: Wallerstein says global trade is rigged: Core rich nations keep poor nations dependent to get cheap commodities.
TOPIC 7.5 • SEMI-PERIPHERY COUNTRIES Describe
📖 Student Book: Page 484 (Core-Periphery-Semi Tiers) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Describe the economic role of "Semi-Periphery" countries in Wallerstein's model and name TWO examples.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Semi-periphery countries are newly industrializing middle-income nations that manufacture consumer goods; they exploit periphery nations for raw inputs but are in turn exploited by core countries for cheap manufactured products.

Examples: Brazil, China, India, Mexico, South Africa (BRICS nations)."
💡 Memory Hook: Semi-Periphery = The Middle Class of countries (Factories, emerging tech, BRICS).
TOPIC 7.5 • ROSTOW VS. WALLERSTEIN Compare
📖 Student Book: Pages 484–485 (Comparing Development Theories) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Compare Rostow’s model with Wallerstein’s theory regarding how countries achieve economic development.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Rostow’s model is an optimistic, linear internal model that assumes every country can independently develop through five stages; Wallerstein’s theory is a structural dependency model arguing that periphery nations are permanently exploited and dependent on the core."
💡 Memory Hook: Rostow = Anyone can climb the ladder! Wallerstein = The core nations at the top won't let you up!
🛢️ Section 3: Commodity Dependence & Global Market Vulnerability (Student p. 486 / Key p. 89)
TOPIC 7.5 • COMMODITY DEPENDENCE Define
📖 Student Book: Page 486 (Commodity Dependence & Trade) 🔑 Teacher Answer Key: Page 89 (PDF Page 97)
Define "commodity dependence" and explain ONE economic risk faced by commodity-dependent countries.
⭐ Exactly What to Write on Your Exam:
"Commodity dependence occurs when more than 60% of a country's total export revenue is concentrated in raw primary commodities (e.g., crude oil in Nigeria, copper in Zambia, coffee in Ethiopia).

Economic Risk: A crash in global market commodity prices or drop in foreign demand immediately causes severe economic recession, government budget deficits, and currency collapse."
💡 Memory Hook: All your eggs in one basket: If oil or coffee prices tank, the entire national economy collapses!
