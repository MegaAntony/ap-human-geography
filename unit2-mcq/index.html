💡 8th-Grade Quick Cheat Sheet: The 5 Big Ideas Made Simple
▼

Here are simple everyday analogies to help you master these 5 topics with 100% confidence:

1. The 3 Densities (Pizza & Farm Analogy) • Arithmetic: Total people divided by total land (general crowding).
• Physiological: People divided by farmable (arable) land. (High = high food pressure on soil, like Egypt!).
• Agricultural: Farmers divided by farmable land. (MDCs have low agricultural density because they use tractors!).
2. Carrying Capacity (The Island Backpack) The max number of people an area can support before nature breaks down. Exceeding it in dry places like the Sahel causes overgrazing and desertification (soil turning to sand).
3. Population Pyramids (Shapes) • Triangle (Wide Base) = Stage 2 (Lots of babies, high youth dependency, e.g. Niger).
• Column / Box = Stage 4 (Balanced families, e.g. USA).
• Inverted (Narrow Base) = Stage 5 (Few babies, lots of grandparents, e.g. Japan).
• Male 20–49 Bulge = Foreign guest workers (UAE / Qatar).
4. NIR & Doubling Time Formulas • $\text{NIR (\%)} = \frac{\text{CBR} - \text{CDR}}{10}$ *(NEVER include immigrants!)*
• $\text{Doubling Time} = \frac{70}{\text{NIR}}$ *(Rule of 70)*
• Replacement TFR = 2.1 *(the 0.1 replaces babies who don't survive to adulthood)*.
5. The France vs. Bangladesh CDR Trap Why does rich France have a higher death rate than developing Bangladesh? Age, NOT healthcare! Bangladesh has a super young median age (few old people to die). France has lots of elderly people over 75.
6. Dependency Ratio Formula $$\frac{\text{Young (<15)} + \text{Old (65+)}}{\text{Working Adults (15–64)}} \times 100$$ Non-working dependents on top; productive workers on the bottom!
