### Question 1 (1.7-Q01)
- **CED Skill:** `Skill 1.A` — *Describe geographic concepts, processes, models, and theories.*
- **AMSCO Reference:** Formal (Uniform) Regions (*AMSCO 2nd Ed, Topic 1.7, under 'Formal Regions (Uniform Regions)'*)

> **Prompt:** Which of the following spatial units represents a formal (uniform) region in human geography?

- **(A)** The State of Montana, where all citizens are subject to uniform state statutory laws.
- **(B)** The commercial delivery reception zone radiating outward from a local Italian pizzeria.
- **(C)** The cultural territory that sports enthusiasts informally refer to as Red Sox Nation.
- **(D)** The electromagnetic broadcast transmission reception footprint of an urban radio station.

<details><summary><b>Reveal Answer & Detailed Rationale</b></summary>

- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Summary Rationale:** A formal (or uniform) region is an area defined by common human or physical characteristics (e.g., shared language, climate, or political boundaries). The State of Montana is a formal political region because state laws and administrative authority apply uniformly within its borders.
- **Why other options are incorrect:**
  - **(B):** A pizza delivery radius is a functional (nodal) region organized around a central commercial kitchen.
  - **(C):** Red Sox Nation is a vernacular (perceptual) region rooted in informal sports fan loyalty.
  - **(D):** A radio transmission footprint is a functional region organized around a central broadcasting tower.
</details>

### Question 2 (1.7-Q02)
- **CED Skill:** `Skill 1.A` — *Describe geographic concepts, processes, models, and theories.*
- **AMSCO Reference:** Functional (Nodal) Regions (*AMSCO 2nd Ed, Topic 1.7, under 'Functional Regions (Nodal Regions)'*)

> **Prompt:** A functional (nodal) region is distinguished from other regional types primarily because it:

- **(A)** Features fuzzy, subjective boundaries that vary depending on personal resident opinions.
- **(B)** Is organized around a central node connected to surrounding areas by transportation or flows.

- **(D)** Possesses permanent, unalterable physical borders defined by natural mountain chains.

<details><summary><b>Reveal Answer & Detailed Rationale</b></summary>

- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Summary Rationale:** A functional (nodal) region is structured around a central focal point or node (such as a city core, airline hub, or broadcast tower) and is tied to surrounding hinterlands by transportation routes, communication lines, or trade flows.
- **Why other options are incorrect:**
  - **(A):** Fuzzy, subjective boundaries define vernacular (perceptual) regions, not functional regions.
  - **(C):** Uniform language traits characterize cultural formal regions, not nodal functional systems.
  - **(D):** Functional regions are defined by dynamic operational flows, not permanent physical mountain barriers.
</details>
