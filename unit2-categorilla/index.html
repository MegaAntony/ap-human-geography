⚠️ The #1 AP Exam Trap: The "Elderly CDR Trap" (France vs. Bangladesh)

Why is France's Crude Death Rate (~9.5 per 1,000) HIGHER than Bangladesh's CDR (~5.5 per 1,000)?

The 100% Score Geographic Explanation: France is in Stage 4/5 of the DTM, meaning over 21% of its entire population is aged 65 and older. Bangladesh is in Stage 3, where only 5% of its population is over 65 and more than half the country is young. Because elderly humans naturally die at higher rates of old age regardless of how modern French hospitals are, France records more deaths per 1,000 citizens each year strictly due to its older age structure!

Visual Guide to DTM Pyramids & Anomalies (Topic 2.3)

Stage 2 (e.g. Niger)

Wide triangular base, steep concave sides. High youth dependency. Needs schools, clean water, and infant care.

Stage 4 (e.g. USA)

Box/columnar shape with vertical sides. Stable growth ($TFR \approx 2.1$). Balanced dependency structure.

Stage 5 (e.g. Japan)

Inverted urn/beehive shape. Base is narrower than middle/top cohorts. Sub-replacement fertility ($TFR < 1.4$).

Guest Worker Anomaly

Massive one-sided male bulge between ages 20–45 (e.g., UAE, Qatar) due to foreign construction migrant labor.

🌾 Topic 2.1: The 3 Population Densities Compared (AMSCO p. 74)

Key concept from Tuesday's lesson: Geographers use three distinct density calculations to assess carrying capacity and pressure on arable land.

1. Arithmetic Density
Total Population / Total Land Area

The basic average. Limitation: Conceals uneven internal population clustering.

2. Physiological Density
Total Population / Arable (Farmable) Land

Measures pressure on food-producing land. Case Study: Egypt has a massive physiological density because 95% of citizens live on 3% of land along the Nile.

3. Agricultural Density
Number of Farmers / Arable (Farmable) Land

Measures economic efficiency. Developed countries (USA) have low agricultural density (tractors/combines). Developing countries have high density (intensive subsistence labor).

  • P — Political: Legislation, voter turnout (gray voting bloc), retirement laws.
  • E — Economic: Tax base, pension insolvency, labor supply, silver economy.
  • E — Environmental: Carrying capacity, arable land use, eco-footprints.

🇨🇳 China's "4-2-1" Trap & The Sex Ratio (Topic 2.3 & 2.9)

China's former One-Child Policy (1979–2015) created two massive demographic crises:

  • The "4-2-1" Trap: One adult only-child must support 2 parents and 4 grandparents with minimal siblings to share the financial burden.
  • Skewed Sex Ratio: Cultural preference for male heirs led to sex-selective abortions, resulting in ~115 boys per 100 girls and over 30 million unmarried men ("bare branches").

⚡ 5-Minute Morning Review Sheet (Before School)

Scan these 5 bulleted categories on the bus or at breakfast right before Mrs. Knight's class.

1. Causes of Aging (Topic 2.9)

  • TFR below 2.1: Replacement level fails because of female careers and urban expenses.
  • Longer life: Sanitation and antibiotics in LDCs; cardiology and Medicare in MDCs.

2. Economic Pressures (Topic 2.9)

  • Shrinking tax base: Fewer working-age citizens paying into pensions.
  • Labor deficit: Shortage of service and healthcare workers.
  • Silver Economy: Boom in retirement travel and companion robotics.

3. Social Realities (Topic 2.9)

  • Sandwich Generation: Adults supporting both elderly parents and dependent kids.
  • School closings: Kindergarten classrooms converted to senior activity centers.
  • Akiya (Ghost towns): Abandoned homes in rural Japan and Italy.

4. Political Maneuvers (Topic 2.9)

  • Gray Voting Bloc: Turnout is +30% higher for seniors; they vote against school taxes.
  • Raising Retirement Age: Governments push retirement from 62 to 64/67.
  • Pronatalism: Subsidized daycare and cash bonuses for newborns.

5. Formulas & CDR Trap (Topic 2.3 & 2.4)

  • Dependency Ratio: $(0\text{–}14 + 65+) / (15\text{–}64) \times 100$.
  • CDR Trap: France has higher CDR than Bangladesh due to age structure, NOT poor hospitals!
  • NIR: $(CBR - CDR) / 10$.
  • Rule of 70: Doubling time $= 70 / NIR$.

6. Map Diagnostic Traps

  • Central Asia: All the "-stan" countries.
  • Polynesia: Hawaii, New Zealand, Easter Island triangle.
  • North Africa: Grouped with Middle East (Arabic/Islam), NOT Sub-Saharan Africa!

7. Densities & SPEED (Topic 2.1 & Knight Notes)

  • Arithmetic: Total Pop / Total Land (crude average).
  • Physiological: Total Pop / Arable Land (High = intense pressure on food, e.g., Egypt Nile).
  • Agricultural: Farmers / Arable Land (Low in USA due to tractors; High in LDCs due to subsistence).
  • SPEED Model: Social, Political, Economic, Environmental, Demographic.
  • China 4-2-1: 1 only child supports 2 parents + 4 grandparents; skewed sex ratio (~115 boys / 100 girls).
